### Changes

- Every icon is now an outline glyph (council round 2): the header actions,
  the reader toolbar, Settings and the rest match the rail's outline icons
  instead of mixing in filled ones.
- The Settings section list is one grouped panel with a hairline between
  rows, in a narrower column that sits right against the open section, with
  no empty band before the divider.
- The shell follows the Ky suite's look (council round 1). The header is
  the logo and an ink "KyPost" wordmark with nothing under it, and the page
  name ("Inbox", "Settings") is its own title below. The rail and tab bar sit
  on the page surface with outline icons and an accent-soft pill behind the
  current item.
- The mail list is flat, like Gmail's: full-width rows with a hairline
  between them, about 72 px tall on the desktop and 64 px on a phone, with
  the preview on one line. The open message's row, the current folder,
  Settings section and theme are accent-soft with an accent bar instead of a
  solid tint.
- Keyword and Mode chips lose their dots; the chosen chip is accent-soft
  with ink text. Cards are flat with a 1 px line and no shadow, switch rows
  no longer fill with the accent, and group labels are sentence case
  instead of spaced capitals.
- Emoji in subjects and bodies no longer show as a missing-glyph box on a
  system without an emoji font: KyPost bundles Noto Emoji (OFL) as a
  fallback.
- Documentation only: council round 1 before/after screenshots in
  [docs/screenshots/](docs/screenshots/README.md).
- Themes are now the Ky suite's six light/dark pairs (ky-ui 0.11.0): Busnes,
  Matter, Ocean, Forest, Ky and Nord. Settings → Appearance has a Mode choice
  (System, the default; Light; Dark) and the six pairs, each previewing both
  halves. Busnes following the system is the default. Tropics, Tropic Night,
  Coffee, Cyber Punk, Neon Purple, Space, Sky and Sun are gone. Matter, Ocean
  and Ky take ky-ui's revised colours, Forest gains a light half and Nord is
  new.
- Danger, warning and success are per theme and separate from the accent:
  danger is a cool crimson, never the rust accent, and Forest's success is
  teal. Errors, warnings and "Paired" badges use them, and the warning banner
  and the Connection pane's last error now show a dot beside their words.
  Every half passes ky-ui's status colour gate (180 checks), now a unit test.
- Documentation only: before/after screenshots in
  [docs/screenshots/](docs/screenshots/README.md).

### Upgrade impact

- A saved theme carries over. Busnes Light or Busnes Dark becomes Busnes with
  that half kept; Follow system becomes Busnes following the system; an old
  Matter, Ocean, White Cliffs, Forest or Ky palette becomes its pair with that
  half kept. A removed palette resets to Busnes following the system. The
  setting is not rewritten until you pick a theme, and picking one stores the
  ky-ui ID (such as `busnes-pair` or `ocean`) under the same key, which an
  older KyPost reads as Follow system.
